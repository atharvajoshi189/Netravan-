🛡️ TigerTrace AI System Architecture Overview

TigerTrace AI is an offline, CPU-native wildlife biometric intelligence system designed specifically for the Pench Tiger Reserve. It replaces months of manual photo sorting with instant false-trigger blank removal, explains individual tiger identifications through geometric stripe minutiae matching, and maps territorial overlap conflict zones.

Phase 1: Triage & Privacy

Eliminates 70–90% blank vegetation frames into staged quarantine and applies automatic Gaussian privacy blur on humans.

Phase 2: Stripe Re-ID

HotSpotter SIFT stripe minutiae matching with visual green connecting lines and human-in-the-loop review queue.

Phase 3: Spatial GIS

Computes Minimum Convex Polygons (MCP) and highlights high-risk territorial overlap and collapsing zones.

Phase 4: Deviation Alerts

Triggers immediate warnings for village dispersal within 2.5 km, territory shifts, and prolonged absence.
