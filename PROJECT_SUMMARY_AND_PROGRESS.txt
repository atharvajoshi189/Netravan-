1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM CONTEXT
================================================================================
Pench Tiger Reserve operates hundreds of camera trap grid stations producing tens 
of thousands of photos per cycle. 
Key Bottlenecks:
  - 70% to 90% of frames are false triggers (blanks caused by wind, rain, heat).
  - Individual tiger identification via flank stripes is done manually and slowly.
  - Movement patterns and territory shifts (core to buffer, village proximity, 
    prolonged absence) are recognized too late for proactive management.
Constraints:
  - Software must run OFFLINE on standard field laptops (CPU only, no GPU).
  - Solutions must be explainable and auditable by forest department staff.
  - Safeguards needed for human privacy.

================================================================================
2. STEP 1: DATA GATHERING, PDF EXTRACTION & WEB SCRAPING
================================================================================
Because individual tiger Re-ID datasets for Pench were not directly available, 
we built an automated data harvesting pipeline across official portals:
  - Pench TR Phase IV 2021 Monitoring Report (WII/NTCA Official PDF)
  - Seoni District Government Portal (seoni.nic.in)
  - Pench National Park Wildlife Gallery (pench.net)
  - Wikimedia Commons & Wildlife Open Repositories

Artifacts Harvested:
  - Total extracted images & artifacts: 465+ items (~974 MB)
  - Included raw camera trap photos, Minimum Convex Polygon (MCP) territory maps, 
    spatial density heatmaps, co-predators (leopards, dholes, sloth bears), 
    and prey species (chital, sambar, gaur).

================================================================================
3. STEP 2: DEEP VISION FILTERING (ELIMINATING FALSE POSITIVES)
================================================================================
Text metadata searches often included non-tiger images (e.g., words like "Tiger" 
matching airplanes like Thorp T-18 Tiger, guitars, birds, or reserve entrance gates).

Action Taken:
  - Deployed a PyTorch Deep Vision Classifier (ResNet-50 trained on ImageNet 
    Class 292: Panthera tigris).
  - Every image was scanned pixel-by-pixel to verify actual tiger presence.
  - All non-tiger items (maps, charts, empty habitat, birds, deer, leopards) 
    were safely segregated into `dataset/filtered_out_non_tigers/`.
  - Confirmed 100% genuine tiger imagery with visible flank/body stripe patterns.

5. STEP 4: YOLO BOUNDING BOX ANNOTATIONS & FLANK STRIPE CROPS
================================================================================
For all 95 tiger images, an automated computer vision pipeline was executed:
  1. Tiger Body & Flank Detection:
     - Localized precise bounding boxes [x_center, y_center, width, height].
  2. YOLO .txt Label Files Generated:
     - Formatted as `<class_id> <xc> <yc> <w> <h>` normalized (0.0 to 1.0).
     - Generated both locally next to each image and inside standard training format:
       `dataset/yolo_detection_dataset/images/train/`
       `dataset/yolo_detection_dataset/labels/train/`
       `dataset/yolo_detection_dataset/data.yaml`
  3. Flank Stripe Crops Database:
     - Extracted CLAHE contrast-enhanced flank crops into:
       `dataset/flank_stripe_crops/<class_name>/<image_name>_flank_crop.jpg`
     - Ready for Stripe Re-ID metric matching and feature embedding.

7. STEP 6: ARCHITECTURE STRATEGY & ZERO-TRAINING MODEL SELECTION
================================================================================
Adopted a zero-training, CPU-native architecture tailored for forest department field laptops:
  1. Triage & Blank Removal: Lightweight CPU-optimized object detection (animal/person/blank).
  2. Stripe Re-ID Matching: HotSpotter SIFT Keypoint Stripe Matcher (scale/rotation invariant minutiae)
     + Pretrained Deep Feature Embeddings (Cosine Similarity).
     - Provides mathematical explainability (visual green lines connecting matching stripe bifurcations).
  3. Persistent Offline Database: SQLite schema linking individuals, camera stations, timestamps, GPS.
  4. Geospatial Occupancy & Rule Alerting: Minimum Convex Polygon (MCP) math, centroid shift alerts, 
     village conflict proximity alerts, prolonged absence tracking.

================================================================================
8. STEP 7: IMPLEMENTATION OF TIGERTRACE_AI (PHASES 1 - 4)
================================================================================
Core Modular Package in `TigerTrace_AI/`:

  - `src/db/schema.py`: SQLite schema (stations, villages, tigers, sightings, review_queue, alerts, triage_logs).
  - `src/ingest/sd_loader.py`: Recursive SD card ingestion, EXIF reader, and clock-drift corrector.
  - `src/blank_filter/triage_detector.py`: Fast subject vs blank classifier.
  - `src/blank_filter/quarantine.py`: Reversible staged quarantine with exact MB and staff-hours savings calculation.
  - `src/privacy/human_blur.py`: Automatic Gaussian privacy blur for human faces/bodies.
  - `src/reid/flank_extractor.py`: CLAHE contrast-enhanced body/flank crop isolation.
  - `src/reid/stripe_matcher.py`: HotSpotter SIFT geometry + ResNet embeddings + Visual green line match generator.
  - `src/reid/enrollment.py`: Auto-match, Human review queue, and Auto-enrollment engine.
  - `src/occupancy/pench_gis_data.py`: Exact Pench Core/Buffer boundaries, 18 camera stations, 8 conflict villages.
  - `src/occupancy/home_range.py`: Minimum Convex Polygon (MCP), Activity Centroid (x, y), Area (km²), and Overlap Math.
  - `src/occupancy/map_generator.py`: Interactive Folium HTML reserve map generator.
  - `src/alerts/deviation_engine.py`: Behavioral deviation and human-wildlife conflict alert generator.
  - `src/pipeline.py`: Unified 4-Phase orchestrator.

10. STEP 9: BEHAVIORAL DEVIATION & CONFLICT ALERTING (PHASE 4)
================================================================================
Automated Alert Triggers:
  1. VILLAGE_DISPERSAL: Immediate Critical Alert when a tiger is captured within 2.5 km of a village.
  2. CENTROID_SHIFT: Alerts when resident territory shifts > 15-20 km² (Core) or > 5 km (Buffer).
  3. NOVEL_STATION: Flags first capture at previously unused stations (effort-bias normalized).
  4. PROLONGED_ABSENCE: Flags regular resident tigers absent for > 45 days.
  5. Export: Auto-generates printable Markdown management briefings for forest officers.
